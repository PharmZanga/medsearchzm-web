Medical Disclaimer

MedSearch Africa provides service-discovery and health-information tools. It does not replace diagnosis, treatment, prescription or advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Emergencies

Do not use the website for a medical emergency. Contact the appropriate emergency service or immediately visit the nearest health facility.

Medicines

Medicine availability, price and product information displayed in the public beta is demonstration data. Prescription medicines must be supplied only after lawful prescription review by an authorised professional.

AI and digital tools

Any future AI-supported result must be clearly labelled, clinically governed and reviewed within its approved intended use. It must not be treated as a definitive diagnosis.
